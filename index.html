Wonder Tech Indonesia is a strategic advisory firm for government, defence, and critical technology. We are engaged at the point where a technology decision stops being a technical question and becomes an institutional one — where being wrong is measured in mandate, sovereignty, or years.

We do not build systems, resell platforms, or integrate them. Our work is judgement: establishing what is actually true about a technology, what it will mean for the institution that adopts it, and what a decision maker should therefore do.

That work lives in the gap between what a system does and what an institution believes it does. Closing that gap before a commitment is signed, rather than after, is the whole of our practice — and the reason our findings hold up in procurement, in audit, and in the room where the decision is finally made.

What We Do

Five disciplines. One question: what should be decided?

01
Advisory · Assessment
Technology Advisory

We establish what an institution actually owns, and what it is actually receiving. Capability assessment, solution and vendor evaluation, procurement conformance review, and second opinions on decisions already in motion. The deliverable is a position a decision maker can defend under scrutiny.

02
Intelligence · Exposure
Cyber Intelligence

Threat and exposure intelligence for institutions operating in contested environments. We assess intelligence platforms against the functions they are meant to serve, examine data sovereignty and jurisdictional exposure, and translate raw intelligence output into terms leadership can act on.

03
Architecture · Systems
Enterprise Architecture

Structure before procurement. We advise on how systems, data, and processes are meant to fit together, expose where they do not, and set the architectural position that lets an institution add capability without adding fragility. We define the target; we do not build to it.

04
Applied · Governed
Artificial Intelligence

Applied AI, examined with the same discipline as any other decision of consequence. We advise where AI genuinely earns its place, establish data readiness and governance before deployment rather than after it, and separate demonstrated capability from the claims that surround it.

05
Strategy · Sequencing
Strategic Digital Transformation

Transformation programmes fail on sequencing, not on ambition. We work with leadership to set the order in which change happens, define success in terms that can be measured, and hold the programme to institutional reality rather than to the roadmap it was sold.

Principles

How we engage.

Three commitments that shape every mandate we accept.

Principle 01
Independence
No stake in the answer

We are not a reseller and hold no exclusive distribution mandate. That independence is precisely why our assessments carry weight in procurement, audit, and board settings — and why we will say so plainly when the right recommendation is to change nothing at all.

Principle 02
Evidence
Findings that survive scrutiny

Every conclusion we deliver is traceable to something observed: documentation, test evidence, system behaviour, or the procurement record itself. Where evidence is absent, we state that it is absent rather than filling the gap with assumption. Our reports are written to withstand challenge from the parties they concern.

Principle 03
Continuity
Senior involvement, end to end

The principal who takes the first meeting is the principal who signs the final report. Where a mandate calls for depth beyond our own, Wonder Tech convenes experienced professionals and subject matter experts appropriate to that engagement — assembled around the problem, and accountable to a single point of judgement.

Dewi Lutfiana Sari, ST., MM.
Founder & Principal Consultant

Wonder Tech was founded on a plain observation: those advising institutions on technology had seldom been inside the systems they were assessing. Our founder had — building banking systems from the technical layer, then leading government-sector technology programmes, including assessments and vendor evaluations across the United States, Europe, and Asia alongside Indonesia's strategic sector teams.

That vantage point now sets the standard for the practice. Every mandate is led personally by the principal consultant, working alongside experienced professionals and subject matter experts appropriate to each engagement.
